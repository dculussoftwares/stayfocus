title: "Blocking engine"
---

The heart of the app: an AccessibilityService that knows the foreground app, a full-screen block screen, and a pure rule evaluator for the 4 rule types. Order inside the epic follows the handoff: **Block now and Take a break first, then daily/hourly limits, then "Use, then rest", then schedules.**

**Exit criteria:** on a real device with permissions granted, every rule type blocks the right apps at the right times, survives reboot and process death, and is covered by evaluator unit tests.
