title: "Accounts"
---

Firebase Auth with Google and email (including password reset), provisioned **only via Terraform from GitHub Actions**. Includes the Account screen, sign-out, and account deletion both in-app and through a web page. The app keeps building and working without `google-services.json`.

**Exit criteria:** a user can sign up, sign in, reset their password, sign out and delete their account. `infra/firebase` provisions Firebase + Auth for dev with zero console clicks.
