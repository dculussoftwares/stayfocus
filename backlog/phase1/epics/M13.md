Each gets a v5 treatment built from M11 components with its shipped copy and a screenshot for design sign-off. Then remove the v2 design system.

**Exit criteria:** no screen uses v2 tokens or components; v2 code, fonts and baselines are deleted; nothing a user could do before the redesign is lost.
