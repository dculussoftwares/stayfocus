title: "Onboarding + permissions"
---

First run: Welcome, sign-in (with "Continue without an account"), the system check for 4 permissions, the Play-required Accessibility disclosure, battery-optimisation guidance for OEMs, and runtime permission health.

**Exit criteria:** a new user reaches Home with all 4 permissions granted in under 2 minutes, and declining any one never makes the app unusable.
