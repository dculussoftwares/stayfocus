## Context
These come first in the handoff order. Take a break is a timed block of everything except the allowlist.

## Scope
- Take a break sheet (`:feature:home`): the RotaryDial with `DialConfigs.Break`, presets 15m/30m/1h/2h, CTA "Start {durationLabel} break" → `BreakRepository.start`, toast "Break started · {durationLabel}".
- Home break card: idle = lime card that opens the sheet; active = live countdown `mmss`, 30-segment progress, and an end-break action (copy from the prototype).
- The engine honours break sessions (BREAK reason) and NOW blocks (already in the evaluator; this story verifies them end to end).
- A "Break over" notification when the break ends (only if the notification permission is granted; channel `breaks`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Starting a 5-minute break blocks a non-allowlisted app and allows the dialer; after expiry the app opens normally.
- [ ] The break survives app process death (state in DataStore) and the countdown resumes correctly.
- [ ] UI matches the prototype; screenshot tests for the idle and active cards and the sheet.
