## Scope
- `WizardViewModel` holding a `BlockDraft` (target, type, apps, period, mins, use, rest, range, now, days, fromAi, aiNote). It is created from a route `prefill`, and its defaults match the prototype `openWizard`.
- Step dots (3; lime up to the current step). Back: step > 1 → previous step, otherwise close. CTA labels: "Continue" / "Continue with N app(s)" / "Turn on block".
- Step 1: 4 type cards (name, description, chip; the selected one has a lime border); tap = select and go to step 2. "Rather just describe it?" → Block tab in AI mode. An ON pills row picks the target (devices are disabled until M8-03).
- Step 2: a 4-column grid of app tiles (icon, name, today's time, check). Selected count. Continuing with 0 apps shows the toast "Pick at least one app". Apps come from `TargetAppsProvider` (this phone = M3-02 catalog).

- [ ] ViewModel tests for navigation, validation and prefill.
- [ ] UI test covering step 1 → step 2 → step 3.
