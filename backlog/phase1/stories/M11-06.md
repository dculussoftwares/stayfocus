## Context
V5_SPEC §4 chart rows. Prototype: `dotGrid()` line 534 (Home hero, child hero), tick progress 180–185 + `viewBlock()` 591, child hour ticks 203–204 + line 620, Insights bars 225–230 + 616–618, ranking rows 239–244.

## Scope
- `DotGrid(values, max, rows = 6, color, highlightIndex)`: pitch 10 (scaled to width), lit r 2.2 @ .85 (highlight @ 1), unlit r 1.4 @ .32.
- `TickProgress(fraction)`: 40 ticks, lit 30 dp orange / unlit 14 dp hairline.
- `HourTicks(hourlyMins: List<Int>)`: hours 6–23 × 3 ticks, height max(3, v/25·34) dp, pink if > 0; axis labels "6 AM", "12 PM", "6 PM", "11 PM" (locale-aware time format).
- `GlassBarChart(values, max, highlightIndex, average, averageLabel, axisLabels, mode = Day | Week)` per V5_SPEC §4 (track, three fill styles, orange glow on the highlighted bar, dashed average line, "Avg …" pill).
- `UsageRow(icon, name, timeText, fraction)`.
- Charts expose a TalkBack summary (e.g. "Screen time by hour, peak 19 min at 9 PM") supplied by the caller.
