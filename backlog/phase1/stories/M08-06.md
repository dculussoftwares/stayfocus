## Scope
- Child: detect a lost permission (accessibility service unbound/disabled, usage access, overlay, notifications) on every heartbeat and on service callbacks → `alerts/{id}` `{kind: permission_lost, permission}`. Deduplicate so there's one open alert per permission.
- Extend `notifyParent` to `alerts` → push "Accessibility turned off on {name}".
- Offline / possible uninstall is **derived on the parent side** from `lastSeen` (no scheduled function): the device card and Remote screen show "Last seen …", and the alert line appears after 24 h.
- Parent: the alert line on the device card, and the red card on Remote with Remind (→ `REMIND_PERMISSION` command → a notification on the child) and Dismiss.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Turning off Accessibility on the child alerts the parent within 2 min on dev.
- [ ] A rules test: a child can't create alerts on another device.
