## Scope
- The list of launchable apps with "{time} · {n} OPENS" and a LOCK / LOCKED pill (prototype `appRows` styles).
- Locking writes a `LockedApp` (MANUAL_LOCK, indefinite); unlocking removes it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A locked app is blocked immediately by the engine and shows "LOCKED" in the list.
- [ ] Screenshot tests for both pill states.
