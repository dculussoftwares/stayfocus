## Scope
- An `AiBlockResult` (kotlinx.serialization) mirroring the prototype `AI_SYS` schema, except `apps` holds **app names** from a fixed well-known list (not the user's installed apps, for privacy).
- `AiNormalizer` (port of `runAi` + `toDays`): unknown type → limit; numbers rounded with a minimum of 1 and clamped to the dial max; period; range regex `^\d\d:\d\d–\d\d:\d\d$`, default 09:00–17:00; weekday/weekend keywords; fallback apps. App names are mapped locally to installed packages (`KnownApps` + label match) → `BlockDraft(fromAi = true, aiNote)`.
