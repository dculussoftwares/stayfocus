## Context
Play's stalkerware policy: the monitoring flag, a persistent notification with a unique icon whenever monitoring runs, and no hidden behaviour.

## Scope
- The IsMonitoringTool manifest declaration with the `child_monitoring` value. **Verify the exact syntax against the current Play Console Help page** and cite it in the PR.
- `MonitoringService` with `foregroundServiceType="specialUse"`, the `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` permission, and a `PROPERTY_SPECIAL_USE_FGS_SUBTYPE` property with the written use case.
- Notification "Managed by {parent}'s phone" with its own unique icon and channel; re-posted if dismissed (Android 14+ allows dismissal).
- Started after linking and on `BOOT_COMPLETED` (verify Android 15+ boot-start restrictions for `specialUse`).
- `:app` gets **no** foreground service.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] After a reboot, the notification reappears without opening the app.
- [ ] The merged `:app` manifest has no FGS or monitoring flag (checked again in M10-02).
