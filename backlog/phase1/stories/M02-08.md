## Scope
- Re-evaluate at range start and end even while the app stays in the foreground.
- Overnight ranges and day chips follow M2-01 semantics.
- Re-evaluate on `ACTION_TIME_CHANGED` / `ACTION_TIMEZONE_CHANGED`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Device test: a schedule starting 1 minute from now blocks the open app when it starts.
- [ ] A 22:00–07:00 range blocks at 23:00 and at 06:59 the next day, and allows at 07:00.
