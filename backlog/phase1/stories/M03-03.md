## Scope
- `UsageRepository`: `today()` (live; refreshed on demand and every 60 s while observed), and `day(date)` for the previous 6 days from the Room cache.
- A WorkManager daily job (~00:15) plus a backfill on app start for missing days (UsageStats keeps detail for only ~7–10 days).
- 7-day averages (mean of the previous complete days that have data) for screen time, opens and unlocks, plus the average hourly line (`avgTotal/24`).
- `blockedToday()` from `BlockEventEntity`.
