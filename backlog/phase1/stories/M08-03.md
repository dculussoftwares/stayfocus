## Scope
- The Kids app declares its own AccessibilityService (`:core:blocking`) and runs `BlockingEngine`. Its `BlockRepository` is fed from Firestore `devices/{id}/blocks`, mirrored to local Room so enforcement works offline.
- Handlers: `BLOCK_APP`/`UNBLOCK_APP` → `LockedApp`; `START_FOCUS`/`STOP_FOCUS` → `FocusSession` (block all except the allowlist until `focusEndsAt`, mirrored to the device doc); `SYNC_BLOCKS`.
- Parent wizard: enable device targets in the ON pills (M4-02). `TargetAppsProvider` for a device reads `devices/{id}/apps`. Saving writes the block to Firestore.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A block created by the parent for the child is enforced on the child within one sync, including in airplane mode after the first sync.
