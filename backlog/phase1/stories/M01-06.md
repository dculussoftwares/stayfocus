## Scope
- `SfEasing = CubicBezierEasing(.2f, .8f, .2f, 1f)`.
- `SfBottomSheet`: slides up in 300 ms with `SfEasing`, 60% black scrim, closes on outside tap and system back, top radius 32, panel background, drag handle. Use your own implementation or a customised `ModalBottomSheet`; it must match the motion spec.
- `SfToastHost` + `ToastController` (via CompositionLocal or injected): bottom-aligned, light background `#F2F4EC`, dark text, 2.6 s, a new toast replaces the current one (like the prototype's `toastMsg`).
- Screen enter transition: fade + 8 dp translateY over 350 ms, as reusable `EnterTransition`/`ExitTransition` values for Navigation Compose.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Compose UI test: tapping the scrim dismisses the sheet; back press dismisses the sheet.
- [ ] Unit test for `ToastController`: a second message replaces the first, and auto-dismiss happens after 2600 ms (virtual time).
- [ ] Previews and screenshots exist for the sheet and the toast.
