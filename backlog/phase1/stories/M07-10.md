## Scope
- `LinkedDevicesRepository`: a Firestore snapshot listener on `users/{uid}/devices` with an in-memory cache. Online = `lastSeen` within 3 minutes.
- Devices tab: a This-phone row; device cards (name, initial, pulsing online LED, today / battery / current app, alert line, badge count); an empty state CTA "Link a child's phone".
- The Home linked-phones strip and the Block tab target picker switch from the fake to this repository.
