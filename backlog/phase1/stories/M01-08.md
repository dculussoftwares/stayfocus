## Context
Used on Home (gauge + 24×5 mini LEDs) and Insights (24×10 LEDs with an average line). The prototype `gauge` and `led()` functions are the spec.

## Scope
- `ScreenTimeGauge(valueMins, avgMins, maxMins = 240)`: a 240° arc from −120° to +120°, track + value arc, 41 ticks (every 6°, major every 10th), ticks lit up to the value, average marker line at the avg fraction, and a centre readout slot.
- Pure function `ledLevels(hourly: List<Int>, rows: Int, currentHour: Int?)`: lit level = `max(v>0 ? 1 : 0, round(v / max × rows))`. Hours after `currentHour` are 0. The current-hour column is flagged.
- `LedBarChart(levels, rows, avgFraction?, axisLabels)`: lit cells in the accent colour, current hour in the text colour, unlit cells `#1F221C`, optional dashed average line, axis labels 00 / 06 / 12 / 18.
