## Scope
- Create `linkTokens/{token}`: a 128-bit random base64url token, `expiresAt = now + 5 min`, `childUid`, model. The QR encodes `stayfocused://link?t={token}` (ZXing core encoder rendered as a Compose `ImageBitmap`, ink modules on a light card).
- "Waiting for scan · 4:52" countdown; regenerate on expiry; observe the token doc and move to Consent when it's claimed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The QR is readable by the M7-06 scanner and by a stock camera app.
- [ ] Expired tokens are rejected (rules/functions test).
