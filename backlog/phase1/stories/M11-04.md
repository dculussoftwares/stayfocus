## Context
v5 shows key numbers as 5 × 7 dot glyphs (`dm()` and the glyph map `F`, prototype lines 512 and 531–533). The prototype also has a `thin` variant (Urbanist 200 at 9 × dot size). Used for screen time, counts, countdowns and the 6-digit link code.

## Scope
- Glyph layout as pure Kotlin (glyphs for 0–9 and `:`; a space advances 2 cells; unknown characters are skipped): returns dot centres for a string and a dot pitch `s`. Unit-tested.
- `DotMatrixText(text, dotSize, color, numerals = Numerals.Dots | Numerals.Thin)` drawing circles of radius .34 × s on a `Canvas`, with intrinsic size = glyph width × 7 s.
- Semantics: `contentDescription` is a readable form supplied by the caller (e.g. "2 hours 33 minutes"); the glyphs are hidden from TalkBack.
- Previews at the sizes used (s = 4, 5, 6, 8, 9) and for "2:33", "23", "52", "14:52", "482 913".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Glyph layout tests cover every digit, `:` and space.
- [ ] Roborazzi output matches the dot numbers in `design/screens-v5/05-home.png`, `16-blocked-app-screen.png` and `20-codes-match.png`.
- [ ] Numbers keep a fixed width per glyph so a running countdown doesn't jitter.
