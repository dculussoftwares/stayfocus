## Scope
- A real `AppUsageProvider`: today's and this-hour foreground ms per pkg = the last UsageStats aggregate (M3-01) + the live in-progress session delta from the engine (UsageStats lags while an app is open).
- The limit applies to the **combined** usage of the block's apps. Evaluate at the exact exhaustion moment while the app stays open (`nextEvaluationAt`).
- Daily resets at local midnight; hourly resets at the top of each clock hour.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A device test: a 2-minute daily limit on Chrome blocks after ~2 min of use (±10 s) and stays blocked until midnight.
- [ ] An hourly limit unblocks at the next hour boundary.
- [ ] Unit tests cover the provider's merge of UsageStats + live delta.
