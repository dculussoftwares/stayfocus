## Scope
- In `MonitoringService`: a heartbeat every 90 s plus on change (debounced 5 s) that writes `battery`, charging, `currentApp` (label), `online`, `lastSeen` to the device doc.
- Upload a usage summary every 15 min to `usage/{yyyy-MM-dd}` (total, top apps with minutes and opens), reusing `:core:usage`.
- Upload the installed launchable apps list to `devices/{id}/apps` on link and on package changes (used by the parent's wizard and the remote app list).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Battery cost measured over 2 h (Battery Historian or the dumpsys summary), noted in the PR.
- [ ] Writes stay ≤ ~1 per 90 s at steady state.
