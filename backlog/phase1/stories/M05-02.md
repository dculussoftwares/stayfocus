## Scope
- Sign in ↔ Create account toggle ("New here? Create an account" / "Have an account? Sign in"), Google button, email + password fields.
- Validation exactly as specified: email `\S+@\S+\.\S+` → "Enter a valid email address."; password < 6 → "Password needs at least 6 characters."; Forgot password with an empty email → "Enter your email first, then tap Forgot password." Errors clear when the user types.
- "Continue without an account" link → permissions; sets `accountSkipped`.
- All auth calls go through the `AuthRepository` interface in `:core:sync`, with a fake implementation (the real one is M6-03/M6-04).
