## Context
All 24 v5 screens are migrated. This story checks the result as a whole.

## Scope
- `docs/qa/v5-visual-review.md`: for each of the 24 screens, the reference PNG and the Roborazzi render side by side (paths), and every difference with a decision (fix now / accepted / follow-up issue). Fix small differences in this PR; open issues for larger ones.
- Maestro: add flows for onboarding → Home, new block via the wizard, AI describe (with the on-device fallback), break start/end, Insights Day/Week; all smoke flows green on the managed emulators.
- Accessibility: TalkBack walk-through of every v5 screen, font scale 1.3 and 2.0, contrast report for white text on orange/pink (V5_SPEC §8 question 3) with exact ratios; fix anything that isn't a design decision.
- Performance: no dropped-frame regressions on Home and Insights (Macrobenchmark or `JankStats` log on a mid-range emulator), noted in the doc.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Review doc complete; every difference has a decision.
- [ ] New and updated Maestro flows pass in CI (`ciDevicesGroupDebugAndroidTest` + Maestro job).
- [ ] Accessibility findings fixed or filed; contrast decision requested from a maintainer in the PR.
