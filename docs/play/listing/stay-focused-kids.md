# Stay Focused Kids: store listing (draft)

**Title (max 30):** Stay Focused Kids

**Short description (max 80):**
Companion app for parents: manage app limits on your child's phone, openly.

**Full description (max 4000):**

Stay Focused Kids is the child-phone companion to Stay Focused. It is for parents and guardians who want to manage
how their child uses their own phone, and it works openly: the child can see that it is installed and that the phone
is managed.

You need the Stay Focused app on your own phone. Install Stay Focused Kids on your child's phone, open it, and scan the
QR code it shows with Stay Focused. Both phones show a matching 6-digit code, and the child's phone asks for
approval before it is linked. Nothing is monitored until that approval.

What the parent can do from their phone:
- Block apps or set daily time limits and schedules.
- Lock a specific app, or start a focus session that pauses everything except always-allowed apps.
- See today's screen time and which apps were used.
- See the child's installed apps, battery level and whether the phone is online.

What the child sees:
- A persistent notification while the phone is managed, with the parent's name.
- A home screen listing the rules the parent has set and today's screen time.
- An "Unlink" button. The child can unlink at any time, and the parent is told.

This app is not designed to be hidden and is not for monitoring another adult. Using it on someone's phone without
their knowledge is not allowed.

Permissions, and why:
- Usage access: to measure time spent in each app, shown to the parent.
- Accessibility service: to detect which app is open so a blocked app can be covered. It does not read or store what the
  child types or sees.
- Display over other apps: to show the block screen.
- Notifications: to show the "managed" notice and reminders.

Data: the app activity above is sent only to the linked parent's account, through Firebase. No ads, no analytics, no
advertising ID, and nothing is shared with third parties. The parent can delete everything by unlinking or deleting their
account. Privacy policy and source code are linked from this page.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
