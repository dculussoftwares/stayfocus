Privacy policy

Draft. This policy is finalised before the first public release (story M10-01).

Who we are

Stay Focused and Stay Focused Kids are free, open-source (GPL-3.0) Android apps. The source code is at github.com/dculussoftwares/stayfocus.

Without an account

You can use the app without signing in. Your blocked apps, schedules, limits and usage statistics stay on your device. We do not receive them.

What we store if you sign in

  • Your sign-in details: email address, display name and a unique account ID (Firebase Authentication).
  • The devices you link, their names, status and last-seen time, the apps you choose to block or limit, and the requests and alerts exchanged between a parent and a child device (Cloud Firestore).
  • A push-notification token for each linked device (Firebase Cloud Messaging).

Permissions

The app uses Usage Access and an Accessibility service only to detect which app is in the foreground so it can block it. That information is processed on your device and is not sent to us or sold.

How it is used and shared

Data is used only to run the features above. We do not sell it and we do not show ads. It is processed by Google Firebase on our behalf.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all data stored with it in the app (Profile, Delete account and all data) or on the account deletion page.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/dculussoftwares/stayfocus/issues.
